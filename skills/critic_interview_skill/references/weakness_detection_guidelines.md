# Weakness Detection Guidelines

Use the following guidelines to identify coachable weaknesses.

## Delivery Weaknesses

### High Pause Ratio
A high pause ratio may indicate hesitation, lack of preparation, or difficulty maintaining flow.

### Many Long Pauses
Several long pauses may make the answer feel less fluent. However, do not overstate this because pauses can also reflect thinking.

### Very Slow Speaking Rate
A slow speaking rate may make the answer feel low-energy or uncertain.

### Very Fast Speaking Rate
A fast speaking rate may make the answer difficult to follow and can reduce clarity.

### Low Pitch Variation
Low pitch variation may indicate flat delivery. Phrase this carefully as a possible delivery issue.

### Low Mean Energy
Low energy may suggest that the response could be delivered with more engagement.

## Content Weaknesses

### Weak Structure
If the transcript lacks a clear beginning, action, result, or conclusion, suggest using the STAR method.

### Missing Specific Example
If the answer is general, recommend adding a concrete example.

### Weak Relevance
If the answer does not clearly address the question, suggest directly linking the response to the question.

## Language
Use constructive phrasing:
- "could be improved"
- "may reduce clarity"
- "one improvement area is"
- "a useful next step would be"
