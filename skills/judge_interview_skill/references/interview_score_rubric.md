# Interview Score Rubric

All prediction scores are assumed to be normalized between 0 and 1.

## Score Interpretation

| Score Range | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| 0.00–0.39 | Needs improvement |
| 0.40–0.54 | Moderate |
| 0.55–0.74 | Good |
| 0.75–1.00 | Strong |

## Speaking Skills
Speaking skills refer to delivery quality. This includes clarity, pace, fluency, and ability to communicate ideas understandably.

## Confidence Score
Confidence score is an estimated interview delivery indicator. It should not be interpreted as true psychological confidence. A lower score may indicate that the response delivery appears less assured according to the model.

## Interview Score
Interview score reflects the predicted quality of the interview segment. It may combine delivery and content-related signals.

## Overall Performance
Overall performance is a broad predicted indicator of how strong the response appears in the interview context.

## Important Limitation
These scores come from a machine learning model. They should be used only for coaching and self-improvement, not for employment decisions.
