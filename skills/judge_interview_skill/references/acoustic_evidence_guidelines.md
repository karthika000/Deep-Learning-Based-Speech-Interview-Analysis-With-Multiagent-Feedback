# Acoustic Evidence Guidelines

The agent does not directly listen to the audio. It only receives numerical acoustic features and interpreted evidence.

## Speaking Rate

| Speaking Rate | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| Below 100 WPM | Slow pace; may feel low-energy or hesitant |
| 100–170 WPM | Normal conversational range |
| Above 170 WPM | Fast pace; may feel rushed |

## Pause Ratio

| Pause Ratio | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| Below 0.15 | Low pause ratio; smooth continuity |
| 0.15–0.30 | Moderate pause ratio; some pauses but acceptable |
| Above 0.30 | High pause ratio; may indicate hesitation or planning gaps |

## Long Pauses

| Long Pause Count | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| 0 | No major hesitation detected |
| 1–2 | Minor hesitation |
| 3 or more | Noticeable hesitation or planning gaps |

## Pitch Variation

| Pitch Variation | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| Below 15 | Low variation; may sound flat |
| 15–45 | Moderate variation; some expressiveness |
| Above 45 | High variation; expressive but may be unstable depending on context |

## Energy

| Mean Energy | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| Below 0.015 | Low delivery energy |
| 0.015–0.060 | Moderate delivery energy |
| Above 0.060 | High delivery energy |

## Required Language
Use careful phrasing:
- "may indicate"
- "suggests"
- "based on the extracted feature"
- "the model prediction suggests"

Avoid absolute claims:
- "proves"
- "clearly shows"
- "definitely"
