# Final Feedback Template

Use the following structure for the final response.

## 1. Overall Summary
Give a short summary of the candidate's interview response based on model predictions and evidence.

## 2. Strengths
List 2–3 strengths supported by transcript, score, or acoustic evidence.

## 3. Acoustic Delivery Feedback
Mention specific acoustic indicators such as:
- speaking rate,
- pause ratio,
- long pause count,
- pitch variation,
- energy.

Always phrase this as feature-based interpretation.

## 4. Content/Transcript Feedback
Mention how well the answer addresses the question, based only on the transcript.

## 5. Areas to Improve
List 2–3 improvement areas.

## 6. Actionable Suggestions
Give practical suggestions such as:
- use the STAR method,
- reduce long pauses,
- prepare key points,
- slow down if speaking rate is too high,
- add a specific result or outcome.

## 7. Next Practice Task
Give one short exercise the candidate can try.

## 8. Safety Note
State that this feedback is for interview practice only and must not be used for real hiring decisions.
