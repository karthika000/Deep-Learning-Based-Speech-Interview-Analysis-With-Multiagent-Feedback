# Strength Detection Guidelines

Use these guidelines to identify positive aspects of the interview response.

## Delivery Strengths

### Normal Speaking Rate
A speaking rate between 100 and 170 WPM supports understandable delivery.

### Low or Moderate Pause Ratio
A low or moderate pause ratio suggests the candidate can maintain flow.

### Moderate Pitch Variation
Moderate pitch variation can support expressiveness.

### Moderate Energy
Moderate mean energy can indicate acceptable engagement.

## Score-Based Strengths

### Speaking Skills
A score above 0.55 can be interpreted as good. A score above 0.75 can be interpreted as strong.

### Confidence
A confidence score above 0.55 suggests good confidence indicators. Scores between 0.40 and 0.54 should be described as moderate, not weak.

### Overall Performance
A good overall performance score suggests the response has useful qualities for interview practice.

## Transcript Strengths

Look for:
- a specific project,
- clear role or contribution,
- problem-solving explanation,
- result or outcome,
- relevance to the question.

## Language
Use careful encouraging language:
- "This is a positive sign because..."
- "A strength in this response is..."
- "The transcript shows..."
- "The predicted score suggests..."
